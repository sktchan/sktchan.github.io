{%- comment -%} Two sizes per photo. The strip shows a 288px-tall thumbnail (~16KB); the lightbox opens the full-size file from data-src. Serving the originals into a 144px-tall strip meant pushing 5.8MB to render 234KB worth of pixels, and the lazy-loading that made that survivable left visible blanks as you scrolled. Thumbnails are small enough to load eagerly, so there are none. The width/height attributes still come from the original: the thumbnail is a proportional resize, so the ratio - which is all those attributes are for - is unchanged. {%- endcomment -%} {%- capture thumbdir %}{{ include.base }}thumbs/{% endcapture -%} {%- for photo in include.items %} {%- assign src = photo.file | prepend: include.base | relative_url -%} {%- assign thumb = photo.file | prepend: thumbdir | relative_url -%} {%- endfor %}
