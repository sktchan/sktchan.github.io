Usage: {% include shelf.html items=list base="/assets/img/books/" %} Each item needs `title` and `author`; `cover` is a filename under `base` and is optional — a book without one falls back to a plate carrying its title, so a new entry is never broken while the artwork is being found. ─── why the title and author are not hidden ──────────────────────────────── The mountains next door reveal their detail on hover because a summit has nowhere to put a label. A cover has the space beneath it, and unlike a peak it is not self-explanatory — the art alone does not reliably tell you what the book is, especially in a 5rem-wide thumbnail. So both lines are simply printed, which also means this half needs no touch fallback at all. Covers are decorative here, not content: the title and author sit right below every one of them in real text. So the alt attribute is empty rather than repeating what a screen reader is about to read anyway. {%- endcomment -%}
