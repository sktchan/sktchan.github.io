I'm a Master's student in Computer Science at Université de Montréal, working with Dr. Sébastien Lemieux at Mila - Quebec AI Institute and the Institute for Research in Immunology and Cancer.

My research focuses on representation learning for transcriptomics. My goal is to advance virtual cell models capable of accurately predicting cellular states under chemical and genetic perturbations.

Before this, my background spanned clinical pediatrics and radiation oncology research at McMaster University. Working directly with patients showed me how often our most advanced tools rarely reach the people who need them most. I want to pursue science to bridge this gap - building scalable, equitable AI for the realities of global healthcare.

Outside the lab, you can find me reading, baking, or in the wilderness shooting film!
